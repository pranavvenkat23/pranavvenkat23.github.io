About

I am a junior at Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology, working across machine learning research and software engineering. My research asks what evidence a model actually uses when it appears to reason. My independent study of the visual cues vision-language models use to reassemble natural and biomedical images is under review at an AAAI-27 workshop, and the NASA GeneLab capstone team I led had its explainable-AI (XAI) study of spaceflight gene expression accepted to ASGSR 2026.

I build software to the same standard of evidence. Emercity, an offline-first household emergency app, runs a deterministic decision engine covered by 462 unit tests; Finance Digest turns Treasury data into a personal federal tax receipt whose line items always sum to the computed tax.
